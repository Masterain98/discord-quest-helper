## What's New in 0.12.1

### Claim rewards in Discord

1. Quest rewards now always require manual claiming in Discord. With CDP connected, choose Claim in Discord to open the corresponding quest. For group rewards, the app guides you through opening each quest and checking its claimed status before continuing.
2. Without a CDP connection, reward actions show a reminder to claim manually in Discord, with a shortcut to client integration settings. Updated reward labels, guidance, and navigation error messages.

### Quest display and Discord connection fixes

3. Completed quests now appear as individual cards, including rewards that have not been claimed yet. Only unfinished quests remain stacked, and refreshed completion states automatically update the groups.
4. Group summaries now exclude completed quests from progress, reward totals, counts, and expiry information, while still including unfinished members hidden by the current tab.
5. Fixed CDP startup when the launcher detects a running Discord client that the initial client scan missed. The app now shows the restart confirmation dialog when that client needs to restart.

### Updates and maintenance

6. Bumped default client config:

    - native build number to `99581`
    - client version to `1.0.9270`
    - Chrome version to `152.0.7977.169`

7. Updated Vue to `3.5.44`, vue-i18n to `11.4.14`, Tauri API and CLI to `2.12.4`, the Tauri shell plugin to `2.4.1`, and rustls to `0.23.46`, along with build, test, and other supporting dependencies.
