## What's New in 0.11.0

### Discover more quests

1. Added Global Quests, where you can browse unexpired quests from different regions and preview upcoming quests.
2. Search by quest or game name, and filter by eligible country or region, task type, reward type, and age restrictions. View rewards, requirements, and start and end times, or open a quest directly in Discord.
3. See your current network region and which quests your account has accepted or completed. The catalog loads in the background, and your last loaded results remain available if a refresh fails.

The global quest catalog is provided by discordquest.com. Check the quest in Discord to confirm whether your account is eligible.

### Manage similar quests together and take control of your queue

4. My Tasks now groups quests with the same name, game, and task types. See their combined rewards, completed count, and average progress at a glance, or expand a group to view each quest.
5. Accept, start, queue, remove, stop, or claim rewards for a whole group with fewer clicks. When claiming group rewards through a connected Discord client, the app opens each quest in turn and guides you through claiming and confirming its reward.
6. Add more quests while one is running. Drag waiting quests or use the arrow buttons to change their order, remove individual quests, or clear the waiting list.
7. Choose Stop current task to continue with the remaining queue, or Stop entire queue to end the run. Before starting the next quest, the app checks its latest status and skips quests that are completed, expired, or no longer eligible to run. If that check fails, the queue pauses until you choose to resume.

### Build your own idle playlist

8. Random idle rotation can now include games only, apps only, or both.
9. Added Custom playlist: search for games and apps, add them to your list, arrange their order, and cycle through them in sequence. Your playlist and selection preferences are saved on this device for next time.
10. Right-click a waiting item and choose Skip this occurrence to skip it once while keeping it in your saved playlist. Incompatible or unavailable items are clearly marked so you can adjust the list before starting.

### Easier navigation, progress tracking, and settings

11. Redesigned the left sidebar to bring My Tasks, Global Quests, Game Simulator, and common tools together. Switch themes, languages, and task modes directly, and check your Discord connection status.
12. The sidebar task icon shows progress and the total task count. Hover to see the current or next task, or click to open the floating progress panel and manage your queue. You can drag, collapse, or hide the panel.
13. Settings are now on one scrollable page, with keyword search, category shortcuts, and a collapsible category sidebar to help you find options faster.
14. Updated quest cards, dialogs, and the Game Simulator layout to improve text and button placement in narrower windows.
15. Software update notification will now be displayed before Discord login.

### Orbs details and update reminders

16. Check your Orbs balance, total redeemed Orbs, and the countdown to your next Nitro Orbs drop from the sidebar, with a manual refresh option.
17. Update reminders now appear before sign-in and on other main pages, making new releases easier to spot when you open the app.

### Discord Console for advanced troubleshooting

18. When connected to Discord in CDP mode, you can enable Discord Console to view live client logs and search or filter the output while investigating issues.
19. Run JavaScript, inspect and copy results, and expand object properties. The editor includes syntax highlighting, command history, and an adjustable height.

### Fixes and reliability improvements

20. Fixed stale Discord icons that could remain in the Windows system tray after the app restarted Discord.
21. Added a cancel option to the Discord exit prompt, so you can return to the app if you clicked close by mistake.
22. Improved quest and Orbs data refreshes when switching accounts, preventing results from the previous account from remaining on screen.
23. Incomplete game and app catalog loads now show an error and allow a retry, helping prevent missing entries from being mistaken for a complete list.
24. Updated dependency package for potential security vulnerability.
25. Bumped default client config:
    - default client build number to `641004`
    - default native build number to `94186`
    - default client version to `1.0.9262`
    - default Electron version to `42.11.11`
